# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The portfolio serves two audiences equally:

- Recruiters and engineering leaders who need to assess seniority, technical
  breadth, career progression, and communication quality quickly.
- Prospective clients who need confidence that Juniel can understand a business
  problem, design a sound solution, and deliver production software.

Visitors commonly arrive from GitHub, LinkedIn, a resume, or a direct referral.
They need concise evidence before deciding to open a project, download the
resume, or start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Present Juniel Filappi as a senior full stack engineer with practical experience
across architecture, backend, frontend, cloud, DevOps, and technical leadership.
The portfolio succeeds when a visitor can understand the profile in one screen,
inspect credible evidence within a few minutes, and reach Juniel without friction.

## Brand Personality

Technical, sober, and precise. The voice is confident without being theatrical,
direct without feeling cold, and detailed only when the detail proves experience.

## Anti-references

- Generic developer portfolio templates centered on cartoon illustrations.
- Rounded SaaS dashboards, floating glass panels, and decorative drop shadows.
- Purple gradients, neon accents, terminal cosplay, or noisy code rain effects.
- Repetitive icon-card grids that list tools without explaining applied work.
- Excessive animation, novelty cursors, or interactions that delay access to
  projects and contact details.
- BMW logos, model imagery, M stripes, licensed typefaces, or other proprietary
  brand elements. The provided BMW analysis informs structure and restraint only.

## Design Principles

1. **Lead with professional position.** State the kind of engineering work Juniel
   does before listing tools or credentials.
2. **Show applied evidence.** Connect technologies to projects, responsibilities,
   migrations, architecture decisions, and outcomes.
3. **Design with engineered precision.** Use a disciplined grid, clear alignment,
   flat surfaces, and a single action color.
4. **Serve two reading speeds.** Let recruiters scan quickly while giving clients
   enough context to understand capability and scope.
5. **Make the next action obvious.** Every route must provide a clear path to
   projects, the resume, or direct contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal compliance certification is required. The interface still maintains
legible contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, useful link labels,
comfortable touch targets, and a reduced-motion alternative as baseline quality.
